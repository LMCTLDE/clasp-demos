      Palette

      Two things. Text needs enough contrast against its background, and colour is never the only thing carrying meaning.

      Read more: Color and Contrast

      Contrast is a number, and you can fix it by nudging

      Paste a colour you use. White text on it needs 4.5 to 1 for normal text, 3 to 1 at 18 point and up. Press Darker until it passes.

      Why it matters

      It is almost always one custom colour reused in a dozen places, which makes this one decision rather than a dozen fixes.

      Try #FFF200, the brightest yellow most people reach for. It comes in at 1.17.

      Colour on its own

      Required fields marked in red only. Press the button to drop the colour, which is roughly what a colourblind student or a greyscale printout gives you.

      Tables

      Not a CLASP letter, but the one that quietly breaks the most. A header row has to be marked as header cells. Bold text in the top row is not a header row.

      Read more: Tables

      Click any cell and hear it announced

      A screen reader reads a cell with its column heading attached, but only if the heading is a real header cell. Switch between the two tables and click the same cell.
